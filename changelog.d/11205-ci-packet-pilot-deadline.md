- `ripr reports ci-packet` now names a pilot deadline in the step summary: when the pilot packet is the timeout partial, First-run status states the cause (the pilot deadline), the effect (no repo exposure or seam packets, so the later `missing_artifact` and `blocked` states follow from it) and the next step. The pilot step reads an optional `RIPR_PILOT_TIMEOUT_MS` (positive milliseconds, passed as an explicit `ripr pilot --timeout-ms`; invalid values are refused), and a deadline or cancellation that lands mid-batch now stores the per-file Rust facts that had already finished parsing, so a retry reuses them instead of starting cold (#11205).
