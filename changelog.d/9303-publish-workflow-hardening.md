- Hardens the extension publish workflow and workflow pinning: the two marketplace publish jobs now declare `environment:` gates (`vs-marketplace-publish`, `open-vsx-publish`, to be created and protected in repository settings), job-level `contents: read`, a PAT visible only to the publish step, and `persist-credentials: false`.
