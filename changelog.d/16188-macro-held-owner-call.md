- A Rust test assertion whose only call of a function sits inside a macro the analyzer does not trust, such as `assert_eq!(check_ok!(pick(0)), Ok(Mode::A))` with a test-local `macro_rules! check_ok` that never expands its operand, no longer gives that function `strongly_gripped` exact-value credit in the repo view. The assertion now reads as an `unknown` oracle for that function. A call written plainly, inside the standard `vec!` or `format!`, or also outside the untrusted macro keeps its credit; a test-local `macro_rules! vec` that shadows the standard name does not (#16188).
