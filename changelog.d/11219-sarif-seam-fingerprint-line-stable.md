<!-- section: Changed -->
- Seam SARIF (`ripr-seams.sarif`) fingerprints no longer move with line or byte-offset shifts: `riprFingerprintV1` is now `rule_id | file | identity_hash | ordinal`, built from the seam's file, owner, kind and expression plus a source-order ordinal that tells textual duplicates apart, so an unrelated edit above a seam keeps its code-scanning alert instead of closing one and opening another. Distinct seams get distinct fingerprint inputs (textual duplicates by ordinal). Analysis `seam_id` and the seam cache are unchanged. After upgrading, existing seam alerts and baseline entries close once and reopen under the new fingerprints; refresh any checked-in `.ripr/sarif-baseline.json` that includes seam results (#11219).
