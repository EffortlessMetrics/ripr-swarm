- A `macro_rules!` wrapper that a file defines once before its calls, and whose definition can bind no name, no longer makes `assert_eq!` and the other trusted assertions inside its calls ambiguous across the workspace. A test that wraps its cases in such a helper macro now pins the owner's return value instead of refusing on a macro binding. Qualified calls, attributed, repeated or function-local definitions, imports of the wrapper's name, binding words or attributes outside the built-in test, lint, `cfg` and `doc` set in the definition or arguments, other macros invoked inside either, `tt` fragments in the definition, and any module or other macro call between the definition and the call stay refused (#6648).
