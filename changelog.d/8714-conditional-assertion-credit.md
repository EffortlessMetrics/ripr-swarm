- TypeScript assertions inside constant-falsy branches are withheld at collection: `if (false) { expect(...); }` and `while (false)` / `for (;false;)` bodies can never run, so their assertions can never discriminate and are no longer credited (the `else` branch of `if (false)` and the always-runs `do-while` body keep collection). Assertions under a condition static analysis cannot evaluate — `if (featureFlag)`, an env read, an identifier — keep their credit, and the result now carries the `typescript_conditional_assertion_credit` limitation naming the unevaluated condition and recording that the credited oracle may never execute; the classification itself never moves (RIPR-SPEC-0243 example 11 stays `exposed`) (#8714).
