- `ripr reports ci-packet` (the generated `ripr init --ci github` workflow) now produces the PR-guidance `target/ripr/pr/check.json` without the 1 MB findings byte budget, so a pull request with more than about 50 findings no longer makes `review-comments` refuse a findings-bounded document. Before, such a PR (4 files, 543 changed lines in ripgrep) got no PR guidance, a `blocked / missing_artifact` first-run summary and, under a blocking `RIPR_GATE_MODE`, a failed gate; `review-comments` still applies its own brief limits to what the author sees (#11143).
