- A test that reads its build environment with `env!` or `option_env!` (for example `env!("CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR")` or `env!("CARGO_BIN_EXE_ripr")`) now keeps credit for its exact assertions. Before, every such test was "not credited" because it "calls `env!`, whose expansion ripr cannot see", though both macros expand to a literal and cannot leave the test; `use std::env;` no longer counts as rebinding the macro either. A local `macro_rules! env`, a non-standard import of the name, and any other unknown macro in the test keep the old reading (#14116).
