- The "Recommended next test" block of the CI job summary (and `ripr first-action`) no longer reports `No first action for suppressed seam` for a pull request that has a review card to act on. A `comments.json` warning that a neighbouring seam is "configured off for strongly_gripped seams" (the default config, so it appears next to any well-tested seam) or an unrelated `suppressed[]` entry used to turn the whole report into `no_action`, contradicting the start-here block above it and RIPR-SPEC-0020. A card to act on is now fail-closed (`gap_state` of `actionable`, or a non-empty `llm_guidance.repair_command`), `suppressed` comes from a structural `suppressed[]` entry (the free-text warning only counts when the guidance has no review card at all), and the selected seam and target skip leading static-limitation cards (#11179).
