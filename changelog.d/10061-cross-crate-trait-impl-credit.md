- A method of an impl of a trait declared in a sibling workspace crate (imported
  with `use rcore::Stream;` or written `impl rcore::Stream for ..`) no longer
  borrows the oracle of a same-leaf-name file's doctest and reads
  `weakly_exposed`: the trait is the workspace's, so with nothing naming the
  method it reads `no_static_path`, as a same-crate trait already did (#9169).
  The trait is resolved from the member crates' own sources when the diff index
  does not hold them, so a member that only re-exports a foreign trait
  (`pub use std::fmt::Display;`) keeps the foreign-trait dispatch allowance, and
  a test that calls the owner keeps its evidence (#10061).
  Not covered, and still able to borrow a same-leaf-name oracle: a glob import of
  a member's trait (`use rcore::*;`), a re-export of a member trait through the
  impl's own crate (`pub use rcore::Stream;` then `use crate::Stream;`), a trait
  generated by a macro, and `[workspace] members` globs that are not a trailing
  `/*`.
