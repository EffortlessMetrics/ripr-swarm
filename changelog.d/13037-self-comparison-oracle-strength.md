- A related assertion that compares a value with a copy of itself (`assert_eq!(b.0, b.0.clone())`) no longer reports a `strong` oracle. It passes for any value with ordinary equality, so ripr no longer credits it as a discriminator: its strength relative to the finding is `weak` and it never confirms observation, like a self-computed expected value. This is conservative for a NaN-capable float or a hand-written `PartialEq` (#16227). A hand-written `Clone::clone` pinned through a derived `PartialEq` keeps its credit (#13037).
