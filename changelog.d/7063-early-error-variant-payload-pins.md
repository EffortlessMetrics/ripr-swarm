- A changed early `return Err(Type::Variant { .. });` beside other error variants is credited by an `assert_eq!` on the owner's result (or on its `.unwrap_err()`) that expects that variant and its payload, so `config-missing-equals-line` no longer reads as a false gap. A variant-only `matches!` no longer confirms a change that may sit only in the payload, and neither does an equality on another function's result, a sibling variant, a hand-written `PartialEq`, a second construction of the variant, or an owner file that imports the enum's variants (#7063).
