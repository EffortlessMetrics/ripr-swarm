- `review-comments` (and the PR comment and CI packet paths built on it) now runs on large workspaces with default settings. The index ceiling rose from 1,200 to 5,000 files and the default `--timeout-ms` from 120 s to 600 s, matching `ripr check`. On ripr-swarm (2,646 indexed files), diffs of 3 to 238 files complete in 43 to 381 s at under 1.9 GB peak, where the old defaults refused or timed out (#12940).
