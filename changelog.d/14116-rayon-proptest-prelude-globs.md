- `use rayon::prelude::*;` and `use proptest::prelude::*;` no longer cost every test in the workspace its assertion credit. Before, either glob was read as one that "glob-imports from outside the workspace, which may bring in a different `assert_eq!`", though neither prelude exports any macro named like an assertion (checked against rayon 1.12.0 and proptest 1.12.0). On ripr's own repository these two globs were the cited reason on 68 of 72 uncredited assertions. As for the other audited preludes, credit needs the lockfile to pin the crate once, from crates.io, at an audited version; any other version, source or spelling keeps the old reading (#14116).
