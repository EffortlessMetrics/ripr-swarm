- `ripr.toml` now fails closed on two silently surprising numeric boundary values, matching the named, key-specific parse errors the rest of the config surface sets. `reports.max_related_tests = 0` is rejected with a named error ("must be at least 1; remove the key for the default") instead of silently emptying `related_tests` in every context packet, and `perl.timeout_ms = 0` is rejected the same way instead of being silently coerced back to the undocumented 30 s default. The explicit `--max-related-tests 0` per-invocation render cap keeps its separate, deliberate contract (#7353).
