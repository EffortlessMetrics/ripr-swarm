- A forwarder that unwraps the owner's `Result` with a handler that only diverges (`self.try_dock(wanted).unwrap_or_else(die)`, where `die` is the one workspace function whose whole body is `panic!`) now hands the owner's `Ok` value through, so a test pinning the forwarder at the boundary reads `exposed` instead of `propagation_unknown`. Closures, substituting handlers (`unwrap_or`, `unwrap_or_default`, `|_| false`), non-diverging, conditional, ambiguous, module-qualified, shadowed or external handlers, and `.ok()`, `.is_ok()`, `.unwrap()` and `.expect(..)` keep the abstention. A unit-returning forwarder is not covered (#9638).
